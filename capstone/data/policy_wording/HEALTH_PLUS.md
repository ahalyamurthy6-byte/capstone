## 1.1 Definitions
This Health Plus policy defines hospitalisation, outpatient treatment, insured person, claim form, and medical report. All terms must be read alongside the schedule so the scope of cover and exclusions remain aligned with the insured plan.

## 2.1 Eligibility
The policy covers named insured individuals whose enrolment remains active and whose records are current. Coverage is provided for eligible healthcare costs incurred during the policy period and in accordance with documented conditions.

## 2.2 Policy period
The validity of cover runs from the start date to the end date specified in the policy schedule. Claims outside the stated policy period are not covered unless specifically reinstated under a valid amendment or renewal.

## 3.1 Hospitalisation cover
The policy provides cover for medically necessary admissions, surgical procedures, emergency care, and related inpatient treatment. Costs are considered within the benefits schedule and subject to required supporting documentation.

## 3.2 Outpatient cover
Outpatient care includes consultations, scans, lab tests, physiotherapy, and day treatment where the person is not admitted overnight. Approved outpatient claims require valid provider evidence and a clear link to an eligible condition.

## 3.3 Sum insured limit
The maximum sum insured for this policy is higher than the standard product and is set in the schedule. Claims are assessed against the applicable limit and may be partially payable where the costs exceed the covered threshold.

## 4.1 General exclusions
The insurer excludes treatment that is not medically necessary, non-covered procedures, unsupported charges, or claims without adequate documentation. The policy also excludes deliberate duplication, intentional misrepresentation, and ineligible provider payments.

## 4.2 Cosmetic procedures
Cosmetic procedures are excluded, including aesthetic surgery, elective treatment for appearance, and treatments designed primarily for cosmetic improvement rather than health restoration. This exclusion applies unless the product wording expressly states otherwise.

## 4.3 Pre-existing conditions
Pre-existing conditions are covered under the Health Plus plan subject to the terms of the policy and the conditions set out in the schedule. This higher tier plan does not apply a blanket prior-condition exclusion for covered medical needs.

## 5.1 Documentation requirements
All claims must include the claim form and invoice, and hospitalisation claims must also include a medical report. Supporting evidence must match the incident date, amount, and insured person details to be accepted for review.

## 5.2 Claim notification within 30 days
Claims must be notified no later than 30 days after the incident or treatment date. Late claims may require additional review and could be denied if evidence cannot be matched reliably.

## 6.1 Fraud and duplicate claims
The insurer will reject claims involving fraud, deliberate concealment, duplicate filing, or misrepresentation. Duplicate claims are compared to prior cases to detect repeated submissions for the same event or claimant.
