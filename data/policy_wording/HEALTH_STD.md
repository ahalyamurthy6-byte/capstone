## 1.1 Definitions
This Health Standard policy defines hospitalisation, outpatient treatment, insured person, claim form, and medical report. The terms apply across all sections of the policy and are interpreted in the ordinary business sense unless specifically qualified in this policy wording.

## 2.1 Eligibility
The policy covers eligible individuals who are named on the policy schedule and whose enrolment is active during the period of cover. Coverage is available only while the policy is valid and the insured person has paid the required premium.

## 2.2 Policy period
The policy period runs from the start date to the end date stated in the schedule. Claims arising outside the policy period are not payable unless expressly included by a valid renewal or amendment.

## 3.1 Hospitalisation cover
The policy pays reasonable costs for medically necessary hospital admission, surgeries, emergency treatment, and inpatient care subject to the covered limits and mandatory documentation. A medical report is required for hospitalisation claims.

## 3.2 Outpatient cover
Outpatient cover includes consultations, diagnostics, therapy, and treatment received without overnight admission. Claims must relate to a covered medical need and include evidence of treatment from a registered provider.

## 3.3 Sum insured limit
The maximum total claim payable under this policy is the sum insured stated in the schedule. The claim must be reviewed against the applicable limit, and any amount above this limit is not covered.

## 4.1 General exclusions
This policy does not cover treatment that is not medically necessary, non-prescribed cosmetic procedures, services unrelated to a covered condition, or any claim not supported by clear evidence. The insurer may reject claims where the evidence is inconsistent or incomplete.

## 4.2 Cosmetic procedures
Cosmetic procedures are excluded unless specifically agreed in writing by the insurer. This covers elective or aesthetic interventions, including plastic surgery, beauty treatments, and non-medical enhancements that are not required for health recovery.

## 4.3 Pre-existing conditions
Pre-existing conditions are excluded under this standard product. Claims arising from any condition known to the insured before the start of the policy or diagnosed before the claim event are not covered unless otherwise confirmed in writing.

## 5.1 Documentation requirements
A claim must include the completed claim form and a valid invoice. Hospitalisation claims also require a medical report. Missing documents may delay decision-making or lead to a request for additional evidence.

## 5.2 Claim notification within 30 days
Claims must be notified within 30 days of the incident or first treatment date. Late notification may result in delay, reduced coverage, or denial where the insurer is unable to assess the claim accurately.

## 6.1 Fraud and duplicate claims
Any fraudulent statement, duplicate claim, or intentional misrepresentation may lead to rejection, cancellation, and referral for investigation. Duplicate claims are treated as a serious risk and are reviewed against previous approved or pending case records.
